# Cold Outreach Scripts — Northbound Dispatch

For phone/in-person outreach to **truck stops, AZ driving schools, and equipment dealers** along the Hwy 400 corridor and GTA. Keep it short, lead with value, leave a card. Goal: get cards displayed and get warm intros to owner-operators.

> Swap in the real phone number. Bring business cards (navy/orange) to every visit.

---

## 1. Truck Stops & Rest Areas (Flying J, Petro-Canada, Hwy 400 corridor)

**Goal:** Permission to leave a stack of business cards / a small flyer at the counter or driver lounge.

**In-person script (to the manager/counter staff):**
> "Hey, how's it going? I run an independent truck dispatch service out of Barrie — I help owner-operators find loads. A lot of your drivers are exactly who I work with. Mind if I leave a few cards by the counter for any owner-ops who are looking for a dispatcher? No cost to you — just trying to help local drivers keep their trucks loaded."

**If yes:** Leave 15–20 cards. Thank them. Offer to check back monthly to restock.
**If hesitant:** "Totally understand — is there a community board or driver lounge where I could pin one up instead?"

---

## 2. AZ / Truck Driving Schools (Barrie / GTA)

**Goal:** Partner to offer dispatch services to graduating owner-operators. Best long-term channel — new owner-ops need a dispatcher immediately.

**Phone script (ask for the program coordinator / placement office):**
> "Hi, my name's Yorkis — I run Northbound Dispatch, an independent dispatch service based in Barrie. I work with owner-operators across Ontario, finding them loads and negotiating rates.
>
> The reason I'm calling: a lot of your grads who buy their own truck hit a wall — they can drive, but they don't know how to keep the truck loaded profitably. I'd like to offer your new owner-operators a simple dispatch option so they're not scrambling for freight in week one.
>
> Could I send over some info, or drop by to leave materials your students could take when they graduate? Happy to do a short talk on how dispatch works if that's useful for your program."

**Follow-up offer:** A free "How dispatch works for new owner-operators" one-pager + guest session. Mention the referral program — schools love passing along a real benefit.

---

## 3. Equipment Dealers & Truck Rental Companies

**Goal:** Cards at the counter; intro to customers buying/financing their first truck.

**In-person / phone script:**
> "Hi — I'm Yorkis with Northbound Dispatch, an independent dispatch service for owner-operators here in Ontario. When someone buys a truck from you, the next thing they need is loads to pay for it. I'd love to leave some cards so your customers have a dispatcher ready to go on day one. It helps them make their payments — which is good for everybody. Could I leave a few here?"

**Value hook for the dealer:** "A loaded truck makes its payments. Happy to be the dispatch resource you point new owner-ops to."

---

## Universal closing lines

- **The hook:** "AI tools scan the boards 24/7 so drivers don't have to. More loads, better rates, less deadhead."
- **The terms:** "No contracts, no setup fees, week-to-week. 8% per load or $200/week flat."
- **The ask:** "Can I leave a few cards?" / "Could I send you some info?"
- **The follow-up:** Always get a name and a reason to come back ("I'll swing by next month to restock those cards").

---

- Business cards (navy background, orange accent bar, white text: name, title, phone, email, website).
