### Posting tips
- **Lead with the local angle** ("Barrie, ON") — owner-operators trust nearby dispatchers.
- **Pin a recent win** in the comments when you have one (anonymized): *"Just put a flatbed on a Hamilton→Sudbury run at $2.65/mi this morning."*
- **Respond fast to DMs.** Speed of reply is the whole pitch — prove it.
- **Don't argue in comments.** Take skeptics to DM.
